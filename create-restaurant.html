🎃 Build Your Haunted Restaurant

Create a restaurant in your group. You decide the name, concept, menu and Halloween food ideas. The menu you create becomes the menu you use in the final role-play.

← Home

1. Restaurant identity

Choose a restaurant name and concept. Decide what kind of atmosphere and food you want.

2. Create the menu

Include starters, main courses, side dishes, desserts and drinks. Give each item a name, ingredients, short description and price.

3. Make it Halloween

Invent creative names and descriptions. Use the restaurant vocabulary, cooking vocabulary and dietary vocabulary from the lesson.

4. Prepare for the role-play

Use your finished menu during your team's restaurant performance.
